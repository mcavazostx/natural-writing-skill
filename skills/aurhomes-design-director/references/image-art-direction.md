# Image Art Direction and Photographic Realism

Use for AI-generated images, image-editing direction, marketing photography concepts, property visualization, and hero imagery.

## Photographic realism baseline
Favor:
- Natural-looking exposure
- Believable lens perspective
- Realistic skin/material texture
- Subtle depth of field when appropriate
- Directional light with plausible shadows
- Controlled highlights
- Moderate color saturation
- Realistic surface imperfections

Avoid:
- Plastic skin
- Hyper-sharpened microtexture
- Heavy HDR
- Neon edge glow
- Excessive orange/teal grading
- Unrealistic reflections
- Impossible architecture
- Repeated AI texture patterns
- Overly perfect staging that destroys credibility

## Prompt structure
Describe, in this order when useful:
1. Subject and action
2. Environment
3. Composition and crop
4. Camera height and perspective
5. Lighting direction/quality
6. Materials and texture
7. Color treatment
8. Mood
9. Text-safe negative space
10. Negative constraints

## Real-estate imagery
- Keep verticals and structural geometry believable.
- Avoid exaggerated ultra-wide distortion.
- Show natural window light where possible.
- Interior improvements must match the requested budget tier.
- For conceptual renovations, do not imply work has already been completed.
- Keep exterior skies and landscaping plausible for the location.

## Human subjects
When reference images are supplied:
- Preserve identity.
- Preserve age and natural facial proportions.
- Maintain authentic skin detail.
- Avoid generic-model substitution.
- Match lighting on inserted subjects to the scene.

## Thumbnail imagery
- Increase clarity through subject isolation, framing, and lighting—not artificial glow.
- Use a simple background or deliberate environmental context.
- Leave deliberate text-safe space if typography will be added later.
